# omarchyvr v0.1.3

Unofficial. Supports the Pimax Dream Air. Not affiliated with Pimax or Omarchy.

Voice that understands whole sentences, screens that stay where they are, steadier head tracking and better eye clicks.

## Install

Download `omarchyvr-0.1.3-x86_64.tar.zst` from the [Releases page](https://github.com/laughtt/omarchyvr/releases/latest), then:

    tar --zstd -xf omarchyvr-0.1.3-x86_64.tar.zst
    cd omarchyvr-0.1.3/ && ./install.sh

Updating from 0.1.x works the same way; your settings are kept. Details are in [docs/INSTALL.md](../INSTALL.md).

## What is new

- **The voice AI decides every command.** With the AI on, every command goes to the model once, as the whole
  sentence, together with what is on your screens. Lara (the small router) runs first and ignores chatter. Long
  commands become one plan of up to 9 steps ("put the terminals on 4, chrome on 2 fullscreen, hide 5"), and a step
  that names no window acts on the window the step before moved or opened. Corrections work in the same sentence
  ("on 3, no, on 4"). New **Listening** setting on the AI page (Quick, Long, Very long): how long Max waits for you to
  finish a sentence with pauses in it. Long is the default.
- **Locked screen layouts.** In Work the screens no longer move by themselves or when you grab them. Pick a layout on
  the Desktop page or by voice: Work 4 (the default), Focus 3, Work 5, Work 6, Cinema, Huge cinema. The layout comes
  back where it was at the next start. **Recenter screens** (or "Max, recenter") brings the whole layout in front of
  you. Apps still move between screens.
- **Head tracking holds still.** When the tracking slides while your head does not move, the app now holds the head
  in place (switch "Hold the head when it does not move", on by default). In the test session this cut an 8-minute
  drift from 4.3 m to 0.34 m. The Tracking page says when it happened: "Head tracking slid N cm without movement;
  held".
- **Gyro calibration only at a real rest.** The gyro is calibrated whenever the headset lies still on the desk for 2
  seconds, never from a worn headset. On a new PC with no saved calibration yet, the app says "Put the headset still on
  the desk for 2 s to calibrate the gyro" until that has happened. Until then a rough value is used.
- **Gaze click v2.** The gaze dot learns from your clicks: each click shows where you looked versus where you clicked,
  and the small correction is kept for your eye calibration ("Learn from my clicks", on by default; it never clicks
  by itself). With "Eyes as a mouse" on, hold-to-refine: the trigger freezes the gaze dot, a small head movement nudges
  it, and releasing clicks at the refined spot (a quick tap clicks at once). **Quick eye check** on the Tracking page
  shows a dot in the headset and tells you how far off your gaze is ("2.1 deg off; now 0.4 deg"). **Steady clicks**
  (on by default): a click stays where you pressed it, even if your hand shakes a little.
- **Max no longer freezes.** A command that copied to the clipboard could leave every later voice command hanging.
  Fixed.
- **The app window always shows up.** If the window process draws but the desktop never shows it (seen at login), the
  app replaces it once.

Prebuilt release: no packaging changes since 0.1.2.

## Tested hardware

i9-14900KF, 64 GB, RTX 4090 (nvidia-open-dkms 610.57.04), kernel 7.2.5-omarchy, Hyprland (Lua config),
Pimax Dream Air on DisplayPort with its controllers, Steam with SteamVR.

## Known limits

- A very slow lean (about 10 cm over 2 seconds) is held like drift. Lean at a normal pace, or switch off "Hold the
  head when it does not move".
- NVIDIA 615.78.08: the ported patches apply and compile, but have never been run with the headset. It still needs
  the "untested driver" opt-in. 610.57.04 is the tested version.
- With the AI on, every voice command waits for the AI, which is about half a second on the tested PC. Settings >
  Advanced > "Fast path: simple commands skip the AI" brings back the instant path for simple commands.
- The tracking and gyro numbers above come from one headset on one PC. Other units can differ.
- The earlier limits still apply: [v0.1.0](v0.1.0.md#known-limits).
